My research studies the interaction between fiscal and monetary policy, with a focus on the strategic foundations of price-level determination and the fiscal theory of the price level. Current projects explore how dispersed information shapes inflation dynamics and confidence crises, and whether the fiscal and monetary institutions built after the 1970s inflation remain adequate in an era of large government debt.
